# Terms for this repository

This repository holds an evaluation protocol and aggregate, non-identifying results of
the OrthoBench benchmark. It contains no clinical images, no patient identifiers and no
patient-level records.

- The protocol and aggregate results are released under the Creative Commons
  Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International licence (CC BY-NC 4.0):
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
- You must not attempt to re-identify any patient or link these results to other data in
  order to do so.
- The clinical images and case-level records of OrthoBench are not covered by these terms.
  They are available only under a separate data-use agreement with the data custodian,
  after institutional approval.
